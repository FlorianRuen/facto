  the real `parseToken` (`export_test.go:4`).

  45 s, 390 k execs; no failure. At `4f7ce66` the seed corpus runs green
  (`--- PASS: FuzzParseToken`, `--- PASS: FuzzRestore_PersistRoundTrip`). The amend only
  rebuilds the same seed strings from a constant; the fuzz bodies are byte-identical.
- Mutation check (60 s fuzz each, scratch copy). Every mutant killed:
